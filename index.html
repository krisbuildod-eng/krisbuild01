Frequently Asked Questions

1. What factors affect house construction costs in Bangalore?
Construction cost depends on material quality, labour charges, location, design complexity, and finishing level.
2. What is the average cost per square foot in Bangalore?
The average cost ranges from ₹1,800 to ₹3,000 per sq. ft depending on specifications and materials used.
3. How long does it take to complete a house construction?
A normal residential house may take 6 to 12 months based on size and project complexity.
4. Do you provide design and planning services?
Yes, we provide architectural design, structural planning, and 3D elevation services.
5. Do you handle government approvals?
Yes, we assist with plan approvals, BBMP/BDA permissions, and required documentation.
6. Can I customize materials and finishes?
Yes, clients can choose tiles, paint, fittings, and other finishing materials as per preference.
7. Do you provide interior services?
Yes, we offer modular kitchen, wardrobes, false ceiling, and full interior solutions.
8. Is there any warranty on construction?
Yes, we provide structural warranty and service support after project completion.
9. Do you provide a detailed cost estimate?
Yes, we give a transparent and detailed cost estimation before starting the project.
10. How can I contact you for a consultation?
You can contact us through phone, email, or by filling out the contact form on our website.
